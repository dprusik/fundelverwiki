# Rolling Thunder

![Item card](/img/user/DND/Magic%20items/assets/rolling-thunder-card.png)

Weapon (Longbow), rare

## Damage

1d8 + 1 piercing damage + 2 thunder damage

## Properties

- Heavy
- (Range 150/600)
- Two-Handed
- Slow

## Mechanics

### Thunder in your heart

As a Bonus Action, you can awaken the runes on the bow. The next attack you make with this bow before the end of your turn is empowered with compressed thunder.

On a hit, the target takes an additional 2d8 thunder damage, and a thunderous blast erupts in a 10-foot radius around the target.

Each creature in that area must make a DC 15 Strength saving throw. On a failed save, the creature takes 2d8 thunder damage, is violently lifted from the ground, and lands Prone for in its space. On a successful save, it takes half damage and isn’t prone.

Once you use this property, you can’t use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.
